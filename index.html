About the data
  • Prices start from the team's Compiled Competitor Research sheet (retail shelf prices at Home Depot, Walmart, Lowe's and others). We added each brand's missing bike locks from its own website (masterlock.com, onguardlock.com, doublelockusa.com, brinkslocks.com, schlage.com) or, for Bell, from Walmart, Academy Sports and Kroger stores. Research checked Nov. 11, 2026.
  • Master Lock's lineup is the Bike Locks collection on masterlock.com (24 products: 17 cable locks, 3 U-locks including one with a cable, 3 folding locks, and one chain lock with a mini U-lock). Six cables in the team sheet are Master Lock products that it doesn't list as bike locks (two are camouflage hunting 2-packs), so they're left out.
  • The "typical competitor" price is the median of each competitor brand's median price, so OnGuard's large catalog doesn't outweigh the other brands.
  • Brand-site prices are list prices, while retailer prices can include discounts. Products with no published price (5 Brinks cables, 3 Bell locks, 1 Schlage cable) count toward the assortment chart but not the price charts.
  • Loop cables without a built-in lock and cable + padlock sets count as cable locks for every brand. OnGuard "DT" U-locks come with a cable. Disc locks, ground anchors and standalone padlocks are left out.

We surveyed — UIUC students about how they choose, use and pay for bike locks, and how they see Master Lock. Security comes first, most students want to spend under $30, and Master Lock is well trusted.

About the data
  • Student Bike and Bike Lock Survey, 140 UIUC respondents, Oct. 2026. Shares are of all 140 respondents. Names and emails are not included on this site.
  • The price, trust-by-feature and branding graphs recreate the team's Tableau and Excel survey graphs.
  • Lock ownership and theft answers were free text and grouped into yes or no by hand. Only 18 respondents own a bike on campus, so results for bike owners are an early signal.

What Master Lock could do next, based on our competitor research. Each insight lists what we observed, the evidence, a potential opportunity and a question to discuss with Master Lock.

1Master Lock doesn't really utilize sponsorships to its best potential.

Evidence
So far they only sponsor 100-foot supermaxi racing yacht, Comanche, officially named Master Lock
Potential opportunity
Dive into sponsoring actual biking events and teams.
Question for Master Lock
What is holding Master Lock from sponsoring?

2Master Lock doesn't promote features to target their target audience.

Evidence
They do not have ratings and features that are easy to differentiate from competitors.
Potential opportunity
Place security ratings and portability features directly onto packaging.
Question for Master Lock
Is there a reason there isn't more on Master Lock's Packaging?

3Master Lock sells many cable locks at nearly the same price, with no clear reason to pick one over another.

Evidence
11 cable locks fall between $9.99 and $21.99, most about $1 apart.
Potential opportunity
Cut the range to three clear tiers (basic, keyed, heavy-duty), each with a visible reason to pay more.
Question for Master Lock
Which of these cable locks sell the most, and are any selling so little that Master Lock could drop them without losing revenue?

4Master Lock doesn't have discounts on their website

Evidence
The discounts and sales that are offered on their products are through their retailers such as target and home depot, their website itself where you can order Master Lock to be shipped to your door doesn't have discounts
Potential opportunity
They can offer discounts on their website itself, sales or promotions. They can also have a system that instead of ordering from the Master Lock website, the website will direct the customer to the nearest retailer where they can pick it up so if they need it soon, it removes the delivery waiting period.
Question for Master Lock
Is there a reason they do not have discounts on their main page?

5Master Lock's UI on their website can be improved

Evidence
On their website they do not have a lot of pictures of their locks or diagrams explaining the features that they highlight in text.
Potential opportunity
They can mimic their competitors or choose their own UI design that showcases their unique features. These reference photos can be added on their website for their popular locks.
Question for Master Lock
Why isn't this already on their website? Is it a part of their marketing strategy?

6Master Lock doesn't offer many bundles like their competitors

Evidence
Their competitors offer bundles with multiple locks and other items.
Potential opportunity
They can offer similar bundles with a cable lock and a Master Lock or other items, this can be tied in seasonally.
Question for Master Lock
Does this go against their pricing strategy?

7Master Lock doesn't offer a mini U-lock for portability needs

Evidence
They have regular sized U-locks but could create a mini U-lock for commuters similar to Brinks' option
Potential opportunity
Master Lock could add a mini U-lock to its product line which should fall under $20 to remain competitive with Brinks
Question for Master Lock
Is Master Lock looking to expand its product portfolio?

8Master Lock doesn't offer a student-priced U-lock

Evidence
OnGuard's cheapest U-lock bundle is about $52, which leaves a gap below it for budget-conscious students
Potential opportunity
Launch a student U-lock priced at $25–$35 to fill the gap below OnGuard's cheapest bundle
Question for Master Lock
Can Master Lock hit the $25–$35 price point without lowering its security rating?

9Master Lock doesn't have a lightweight combination bike lock

Evidence
OnGuard's secure locks are heavy and mostly keyed, and Master Lock is already the familiar school-locker combination brand
Potential opportunity
Create a light combination bike lock that uses students' existing familiarity with Master Lock combination locks
Question for Master Lock
Would students see a combination bike lock as secure as a keyed one?

10Competitors aren't marketing bike locks to students. OnGuard markets toughness rather than targeting students

Evidence
College students and their families planned a record $103.5B in back-to-school spending in 2026 (about $1,438 each), and 25% plan to shop at college bookstores
Potential opportunity
Take advantage of the back-to-school season with campus bookstore and move-in promotions, such as a bike lock bundled with a dorm padlock
Question for Master Lock
Which campus bookstores or retailers would Master Lock need to partner with to run these promotions?

11Master Lock doesn't have safety ratings

Evidence
People aren't able to discern what level of security that they are paying for at their desired price point
Potential opportunity
Partner with a company or U.S firm that tests bike locks so people can figure out what level of security they are paying for
Question for Master Lock
What is holding Master Lock from partnering with companies to do this and what does the cost of it look like?

12Master Lock has less competition at Lowe's and Target

Evidence
Schlage isn't sold at either store, so Master Lock only competes with Kryptonite there
Potential opportunity
Focus on getting more shelf space and promotions at Lowe's and Target
Question for Master Lock
Why isn't Schlage sold at Lowe's or Target?

13Master Lock's cable locks cost more than Schlage's

Evidence
Schlage's 15 ft cable is cheaper than Master Lock's for the same length, and its 5 ft keyed cable costs almost half as much
Potential opportunity
Lower the price of Master Lock's cable locks to compete with Schlage
Question for Master Lock
Can Master Lock lower these prices without losing too much profit?

14Master Lock doesn't have clear security labels

Evidence
Schlage doesn't label its locks by security level either, so buyers can't see an upgrade path
Potential opportunity
Label locks as Basic, High, or Max so buyers know what level of security they're paying for
Question for Master Lock
What would a lock need to have to count as Basic, High, or Max?
